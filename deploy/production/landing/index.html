Why alomails

A workspace that's yours, not rented.

The convenience of the big providers, without handing them your correspondence. alomails is built on the promise that your mail is yours alone.

Private & sovereign

Your mail lives in Europe, on infrastructure that answers to European law — not to an advertising business. Nothing is scanned to profile you, and nothing is ever sold.

Mail and calendar, together

Write, schedule, and invite in one place. Send real invitations, RSVP to the ones you receive, and handle repeating events cleanly — the way Outlook and Gmail work, without the watching.

A real desktop app

Not a browser tab — an installed app with its own window and icon, on Windows and macOS, that keeps itself up to date automatically. Prefer the web? The same alomails is one click away.

Yours, with no lock-in

Open standards — IMAP for mail, CalDAV and CardDAV for calendar and contacts — mean your phone and other apps just work. And you can leave whenever you like, taking everything with you.
